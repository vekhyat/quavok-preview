Discuss Your Workflow

You know the work. We make it flow.

Start with one process that gets in the way of your real work. We’ll listen, design it with you, build it and keep it running. You decide what good looks like. We handle the technology.

Book a Discovery Call

  1. 01Stage 1 of 4

    Discovery

    A conversation about one process that's taking your team's time. We learn how it works today, which tools it touches, where it stalls and what a good outcome looks like. We'll tell you honestly if it isn't a good candidate for automation.

    What we need from you
    A call, and a few real examples of the work.
    What we do
    Questions, a map of the process, a view on what to automate first.
    You come away with
    A shared understanding of the process and a proposal.

  2. 02Stage 2 of 4

    Design

    We write down exactly what the automation will do: what starts it, which steps run on their own, where it waits for your approval, what goes to a person, and which tools it connects to. Nothing is built until you agree.

    What we need from you
    Read the scope, ask questions, approve it.
    What we do
    Write the scope in plain language and check every integration.
    You come away with
    An approved scope.

  3. 03Stage 3 of 4

    Build

    We build the agents, workflows and integrations, then test them against real examples from your business. You see the results and the messages it will send before anything runs for real.

    What we need from you
    Review test results and approve go-live.
    What we do
    Build, connect, test and fix.
    You come away with
    A tested automation, ready to switch on.

  4. 04Stage 4 of 4

    Deploy & ongoing management

    We deploy it and run it. We monitor it, fix problems, keep it current when your tools or process change, and add new automations when you ask. Each customer's automations are intended to run in their own dedicated, managed cloud environment.

    What we need from you
    Approve what needs you. Tell us what's changed.
    What we do
    Operate, monitor, maintain and extend.
    You come away with
    An automation we run, with you in charge.

Once it's running

It waits for you where you want a say.

Automations work through the agreed steps on their own. Where you asked to approve something, they stop and wait. Where something falls outside the rules, they hand it to a person.

  • Clear summaries of what ran and what is waiting on you.
  • Exceptions go to your team instead of being guessed at.
  • We monitor, investigate failures and fix the cause.

When something goes wrong

Automations are designed to stop and hand over rather than push on. If a step fails, a tool is unavailable, or an item doesn't fit the rules, it is held or routed to a person, and we look into it. You're told when something needs your attention.

FAQ

About the process.

Do I need technical knowledge?

No. You explain how the work happens today and what a good result looks like. We handle the design, build, integrations, hosting and maintenance. You review and approve in plain language.

Who creates and maintains the agents?

We do. The Quavok team designs, builds, tests, deploys, monitors and maintains every agent and workflow. You never build, configure or deploy anything yourself, and there is no builder to learn.

Can I request additional automations?

Yes. Tell us what else is eating your team’s time, by email, on a call, or through Rocket, which forwards the request to us. We scope it with you, you approve it, and we build and run it alongside what’s already running.

How does pricing work?

Every build is different, so we don’t publish plans or prices. After a discovery call we’ll propose a price for building your automation and for running it, before any work starts. There is no charge for the discovery call.

What happens when an automation fails?

Running it is our job. We monitor the automations we run, and they are designed to stop and hand over rather than guess: when something fails or falls outside the agreed rules, the item is held or routed to a person, and we investigate and fix the cause.
