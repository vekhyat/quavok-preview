Rocket demo speech generated locally using Piper TTS (OHF-Voice/piper1-gpl) and the en_US-ljspeech-medium voice.
Model: https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices/tree/main/en/en_US/ljspeech/medium
Model card: dataset is the public-domain LJ Speech Dataset, https://keithito.com/LJ-Speech-Dataset/ .
Only generated audio is shipped. Piper, the model and generation dependencies are not part of the website runtime.
Keyboard switch samples are original procedural Foley created for this website.
